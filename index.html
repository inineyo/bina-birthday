chapter 08

happy birthday, sayangku

i don't really know where to start, because even after everything i've written about you, i still feel like there's so much more i want to say.

i'm really happy i met you, bina. we started with something so simple, just talking about music and random things, until you became someone i look for every day. now, i can't really imagine going through a whole day without talking to you.

i want to keep hearing about your day. i want to know what made you laugh, what annoyed you, what you've been thinking about, or even the most boring thing that happened. i want to hear all of it. i like knowing what's going on in your life, even when it's just something small.

i want to keep having conversations with you that go on for hours, calling you for no reason, playing games together, watching things together, laughing at things that probably aren't even that funny, and just being ourselves. i don't need anything big. i just like having you there.

and whenever you need someone, i want you to know that you can come to me. i'll listen to your stories, your complaints, your worries, everything. i might not always know what to say or how to fix things, but i'll always try to be there for you.

thank you for choosing me and for letting me be a part of your life. thank you for all the care you give me, for listening to me, and for making me feel loved in so many different ways.

for this new year of your life, i hope you get more reasons to be happy. i hope you get to experience things you've been waiting for, reach the goals you've been working on, and meet people who always treat you with the kindness you deserve.

i hope you become more confident in yourself and in the things you can do. i hope you never feel like you have to be perfect to be loved or appreciated. i hope you keep growing into the person you want to be, at your own pace.

when things get hard, i hope you remember that you don't have to handle everything alone. i hope you always have someone to listen to you, support you, and remind you that you're doing better than you think.

most of all, i hope you're happy. not just today because it's your birthday, but on ordinary days too. i hope there are more days where you wake up excited about something, laugh until your stomach hurts, and go to sleep feeling like it was a good day.

and selfishly, i hope i get to be there for a lot of those days.

happy birthday, bina.
